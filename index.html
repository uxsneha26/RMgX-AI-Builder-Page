05 — How we build

Six steps. One team, start to finish.

Designers, engineers and data specialists work together from the first workshop to live operations, so nothing gets lost in hand-offs.

  1. 01 · 1–2 weeks

    Discover

    Users, workflows, data and constraints. We agree what success looks like and how to measure it.

  2. 02 · 1–2 weeks

    Define

    Prioritised use cases, success metrics, an architecture outline and guardrails.

  3. 03 · 2–4 weeks

    Design

    Flows and prototypes on real model output, tested with the people who’ll use them.

  4. 04 · 6–12 weeks

    Engineer

    Production code, integrations, evaluation and security, shipped in short releases.

  5. 05 · 1–2 weeks

    Deploy

    Staged rollout, monitoring and team onboarding. No big-bang launches.

  6. 06 · ongoing

    Optimise

    Track quality, cost and adoption, then tune prompts, retrieval and models as usage grows.
